Map

You see jobs nearby

The posting shows the trade, the area, and the hours. It is not a WhatsApp group where the message disappears. If you are in Queens, the map shows what is around you, not a list from another state.

  • You filter by trade.
  • You see the distance to the job.
  • You open the posting and write from there.

Trust

Ratings, and the chat stays on the job

The profile shows the rating and the reviews. When someone writes, the conversation and the posting stay together. Pay arrives in one or two days and stays on that same job.

  • The rating is visible before you accept.
  • The chat does not mix with other postings.
  • You check in when you arrive and when you leave.

Before you download

Is it free?

Downloading it is free, on iPhone and Android. The button above opens the store on your phone.

Which cities?

New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. Inside the app the map shows the distance to each job.

How do I get paid?

The contractor pays from the app when the job closes. The money arrives in one or two days and the record stays on that posting.
