Privacy

This copy of the site does not open accounts and does not store the form on a server. When you press Send, the message is prepared in the browser and you can open an email to info@miploy.com.

What the form asks for

Name, phone, whether you are a worker or a contractor, city, trade or role, and the message. That information leaves this page only if you open the email and send it.

The app

MiPloy's public App Store listing says the account may be linked to location, contact details, and usage. The text that governs that handling has to be published by the company. This draft does not replace it.
