Post, choose, and pay in the same app

Each step sits next to one screen. The sample cards are not real people or real jobs.

Step 1

You post and see who is nearby

Trade, area, how many people, and when. The map marks available people around the job and you set the search radius.

The posting reaches people who do that trade inside the radius. It does not depend on a message group.

Sample posting

2 drywall people · Queens This week · 7:00–3:30

Step 2

You review the application

You see the trade, the distance, and the notes. From that card you message or hire.

Photos of finished work sit on the same profile as the rating.

What you check

Trade, distance, and photos Before you invite

Step 3

You check hours and pay in the same app

Clock-in and clock-out stay on the posting. Those hours organize the pay.

The in-app fee is 15%. The market figure the brand uses as a reference is 30%. The worker is paid in one or two days.

On the same posting

Clock-in, clock-out, and pay 15% fee · paid in 1 or 2 days
