Questions about the app

Is the app free?

Downloading it is free on the App Store and Google Play. The person hiring pays a 15% fee inside the app.

Which cities?

New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut.

Which trades?

Construction, drywall, roofing, plumbing, electrical, painting, carpentry, and landscaping.

How does pay work?

The contractor pays from the app. The worker is paid in one or two days. The hours come from clock-in and clock-out.

How fast do you reply?

The same business day, Monday to Friday, between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.
