How it works

Two paths. The same phone.

One person comes in looking for work. The other comes in to fill a role. Both sign up with email or phone and meet on the map.

If you need a crew

Three steps. The last one opens the store.

1. You post

You say which trade you need, where the job is, how many people, and the radius.

The posting goes to people already nearby who do that trade.

Sample posting

2 drywall people · Queens This week · 7:00–3:30

2. You review

You look at the trade, the distance, the notes, and the portfolio before you write or hire.

What you check

Trade, distance, and photos Before you invite

3. You close the job

The hours on the check-in organize the pay. The in-app fee is 15% and the pay arrives in one or two days.

On the same posting

Clock-in, clock-out, and pay 15% fee · paid in 1 or 2 days

If you want work

The other path. It also ends at the store.

1. You sign in

Phone or email. Google or Apple too. That is where you say you are looking for work.

2. You open the map

Each area shows postings in your trade around where you are.

3. You start

You talk in the job chat, check in when you arrive, and the pay stays in the app.

Where do I start if I do not yet know whether I will work or hire?

You download the app and pick a side when you create the account. If you want a person to walk you through it, contact hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
